Wedding day

Bridal Makeup

Full wedding-day makeup application, built from a shade-matched base in either airbrush or HD, and finished to hold through vows, heat, tears and the last dance. We test every product on your skin at the trial first — nothing new touches your face on the day itself.

Your artist arrives with a 48-shade foundation kit, false lash options, and a touch-up bag that stays on-site for the full booking window. Coverage typically runs six to eight hours depending on your ceremony and reception schedule.

Best paired with a trial four to six weeks out so undertone, coverage level and lip colour are locked before the week of the wedding.

  • 90-minute consultation and skin prep at booking
  • Airbrush or HD full-face application, your choice
  • False lashes and long-wear setting spray included
  • On-site touch-up kit for the full coverage window
  • Complimentary patch test scheduled two weeks prior
  • Artist stays through the first dance for one final check

From $890

6–8 hour coverage window. Price varies with airbrush upgrade, guest-count travel and early-call timing.

Hairstyling

Updos, soft waves, braided crowns and sleek chignons, engineered around your veil, dupatta or headpiece from the first pin. We work with your natural texture and density rather than fighting it, so the style survives humidity, dancing and a car door on the way to the reception.

Every style is pinned with a two-hour heat-and-humidity test at the trial, and we bring backup pins, extensions and texture spray to the wedding-day kit as standard.

Fresh floral or jewellery placement is coordinated with your makeup artist so the finished look reads as one styling, not two separate appointments.

  • Trial styling with photos in three light conditions
  • Veil, dupatta or headpiece placement included
  • Backup pins, extensions and texture spray on-site
  • Fresh floral pinning coordinated with your florist
  • Humidity-tested hold for outdoor ceremonies

From $520

2–3 hour styling session. Price varies with hair length, extensions and travel distance.

Pre-wedding

Mehendi

Traditional Rajasthani, Arabic and contemporary minimal designs, applied freehand with organic henna cones for a deep, long-lasting stain. Zainab, our lead mehendi artist, sketches your motif options in advance so the design is agreed before the cone ever touches skin.

Standard bridal coverage runs hands to mid-forearm on both arms and takes three to five hours depending on density. Full arm-to-elbow and matching foot designs are available as an upgrade.

We use only natural, PPD-free henna paste and provide aftercare instructions to deepen and protect the stain for up to three weeks.

  • Design consultation and motif sketch beforehand
  • Hands-to-forearm coverage on both arms, standard
  • Natural, PPD-free henna paste — sensitive-skin friendly
  • Aftercare kit with lemon-sugar sealant included
  • Foot design and full-arm upgrade available

From $340

3–5 hour application. Price varies with design density, foot coverage and number of family members.

Before the day

Trial Makeup

A two-hour rehearsal for brides who want to decide on a look before the wedding week begins. We build one full face and one hairstyle direction together, then photograph the result in daylight and tungsten light so you can see exactly how it will read on camera.

Bring reference photos, your dress swatch and any jewellery you plan to wear — colour-matching against the actual pieces makes a measurable difference to the final look.

Most brides book this four to eight weeks before the wedding, which leaves time to adjust anything before the final booking is confirmed.

  • Full consultation and mood-board review
  • Complete makeup application, one revision included
  • One hairstyle direction tested alongside the makeup
  • Daylight and tungsten photo tests to keep for reference

From $240

One 2-hour in-studio session. Credited toward The Bride package if booked within 30 days.

Pre-wedding

Engagement Styling

A lighter, dewier register than wedding-day makeup — built for ring ceremonies, roka functions and engagement photography rather than an eight-hour wear window. Skin is left visibly luminous rather than fully matte, which photographs better under the softer, often indoor lighting these functions use.

We also direct outfit, jewellery layering and drape for the sitting, so the finished photographs feel styled rather than simply made-up.

Hair is typically softer and looser than the wedding-day style — think undone waves or a low twist rather than a structured updo.

  • Dewy, camera-ready makeup application
  • Soft hairstyling — waves, twist or half-up direction
  • Outfit, jewellery and drape styling guidance
  • Indoor and outdoor lighting tested at consultation

From $610

3–4 hour session including hair. Price varies with number of look changes and travel.

The second act

Reception Styling

A full look change between ceremony and party — smokier eyes, a bolder lip and a sleeker hair silhouette that reads under dance-floor lighting the way daytime softness does not. Most brides schedule this as a twenty-minute changeover during cocktail hour, done in a side room near the reception space.

The base from your wedding-day makeup is refreshed and rebuilt rather than removed, which keeps the change fast without compromising wear-time for the rest of the night.

Hair is typically taken down from an updo into loose waves, or tightened into a sleeker low bun, depending on the dress change.

  • Full look-change consultation at the trial
  • Smokier eye and bolder lip re-application
  • Hair re-styled to match the reception silhouette
  • Twenty-minute on-site changeover during cocktail hour
  • Touch-up kit left with the wedding party for the night

From $740

Booked as an add-on to wedding-day coverage. Price varies with changeover complexity and venue distance.
