Terms & Privacy

Bookings and deposits

A 25% deposit reserves your date and your named artist. The deposit is credited in full against your final balance and is never charged as a separate fee. The remaining balance is split across two installments — 50% at 30 days before the date, and the remainder on the day of the first service.

Cancellation and rescheduling

Dates may be moved free of charge up to 60 days before the booking, subject to availability. Inside 60 days the deposit is non-refundable, but may be transferred once to a new date within twelve months.

Your data

This is a front-end template. No account details leave your browser — everything you enter is stored in this device's localStorage and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.
