How it works

Payment terms

Straightforward, in writing, before you ever hand over a card.

25% deposit holds the date

A 25% deposit reserves your date and your artist on our calendar. It is applied in full to your final balance — never an extra fee.

Balance in two installments

The remaining balance splits in two: 50% due 30 days before your date, and the final 25% due on the wedding day itself.

Free rescheduling

Dates move. Reschedule at no charge up to 60 days out, subject to your artist's availability on the new date.

We accept bank transfer, Zelle, Venmo and all major credit cards. A 3% processing fee applies to card payments on balances over $1,000; deposits, bank transfer and cash are always fee-free.
Common questions

Pricing questions, answered

Your 25% deposit reserves your date and locks in the specific artist assigned to you — we do not double-book a date once a deposit clears. The full deposit amount is credited toward your final balance; it is never charged as a separate fee.

Listed prices exclude local sales tax. Tax is calculated at checkout based on your service address and shown as a separate line on your invoice before you pay.

Peak season runs May through October and all of December — our busiest wedding months. The 15% surcharge applies to the three packages above and reflects the additional staffing we bring on to keep one artist per bride, even in our heaviest weeks.

Travel within 40 miles of San Francisco is included in The Bride and The Full Week. Beyond that, we quote flights, lodging and local transport for the artist team at cost, with no markup — you see the exact figure before you book, whether it's Napa or Udaipur.

Cancel more than 60 days before your date and your deposit converts to studio credit, valid for 12 months. Cancel inside 60 days and the deposit is non-refundable, since we have already turned away other dates for your artist.

Yes. Move from The Trial to The Bride, or The Bride to The Full Week, any time up to 45 days before your date — whatever you have already paid is credited in full toward the new package.
